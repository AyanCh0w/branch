How is this different from everyone using ChatGPT separately?

Branch puts your team’s sources and AI conversations on one shared canvas. Teammates can continue the same chat or branch into separate questions, carrying the earlier context along. The team can see how the ideas connect without copying answers between private chats.

Can several people work in the same chat?

Yes. Teammates share the conversation and draft, and responses sync to the board. If another person is actively editing that chat, Send waits so their changes aren’t missed. People can work in different chat nodes at the same time.

What carries over when I branch?

The conversation up to the branch point and its connected upstream context. Other branches stay independent until you explicitly connect them. Attached source snapshots stay fixed until you refresh them.

What can I bring in as context?

Notes, text files, PDFs, supported images, website links with your notes, and available public YouTube captions. Websites are looked up when you send; blocked or private pages may be unavailable. Video context uses captions, not unseen visuals.

Can I use it solo or with different teams?

Both. Start in your personal workspace, create team workspaces for different groups, and organize boards by project. Owners can move boards between their personal space and a team they belong to.

Who can access a board?

Board owners manage editors, viewers, and revocable guest links. Team members can edit their team’s boards. Invitees need access to the running Branch app to open the link.
