A few answers.

Can several people work in the same chat?

Yes. Teammates share the conversation and draft, and responses sync to the board. People can work in different chats at once. If someone else is editing a chat, Send waits so their changes are not missed.

What carries over when I branch?

The conversation up to that point and its connected context. Other branches stay separate until you connect them. Attached source snapshots stay fixed until you choose to refresh them.

What can I add?

Notes, PDFs, images, website links, and public YouTube captions. Websites are looked up when you send; private or blocked pages may be unavailable.

Who can access my board?

Owners manage editors, viewers, and revocable guest links. Team members can edit their team’s boards. You can also keep a board in your personal workspace and invite collaborators when you’re ready.
