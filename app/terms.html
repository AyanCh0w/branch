These general policies describe the current early-access service. Operator and support contact details are still being finalized; this draft will be updated before a broader launch.

Use the service only if you can enter an agreement under applicable law or have any required parent, guardian, or organizational permission. If you use Branch for a team or organization, you must have permission to act on its behalf.

Provide accurate account information, keep your sign-in credentials secure, and use only accounts and workspaces you are authorized to access. You are responsible for activity you authorize through your account. Board owners and team administrators control membership and sharing. Editors, viewers, and invited guests may have different permissions.

Check a board’s audience before adding information. A collaborator may copy or export content they can access. Removing access does not recall copies already made.

Only upload or share material you have the right to use. You are responsible for obtaining permission from other people before adding their private information. Keep independent copies of important work.
